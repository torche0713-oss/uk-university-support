UK University Support is a free guidance service designed to help eligible UK residents who feel overwhelmed by the university application process. We are not a recruitment agency, not an admissions body, and not affiliated with UCAS or any university.

What We Do

Many UK residents who would like to go to university find themselves stuck — unsure what documents they need, overwhelmed by deadlines, or simply too busy with work and family to manage the process alone. We help by providing:

  • A free 15-minute planning call to understand your situation
  • A personal admission consultant assigned to guide you
  • A tailored application checklist and step-by-step timeline

Who We Serve

Our service is for UK residents who have lived in the UK for the last 3 years and are at least 21 years old. Whether you are looking to start a degree for the first time or change to a new course, we are here to help you map out your next steps.

Our Approach

We believe university applications should not feel overwhelming. Our approach is simple: listen to your situation, provide clear guidance, and give you a personalised plan so you know exactly what to do next. No pressure, no hidden costs, no obligation.
